Research

Research

My research asks how network structures can be modeled, reconstructed, and generated in ways that preserve meaningful social and structural patterns.

Signed networks

Reconstruction & modeling

How can signed relations be reconstructed from observed data while preserving the balance and structural properties that matter in real systems?

Synthetic data

Realistic network generation

How can synthetic networks reproduce important statistics and local/global patterns well enough for benchmarking and downstream learning?

Network analysis

Structural questions

Counting triangles, studying link structure, and characterizing distributions provide lenses for understanding complex relational systems.

Predictive modeling

Downstream applications

Generated and reconstructed networks can support predictive tasks such as link prediction and node-level analysis.
