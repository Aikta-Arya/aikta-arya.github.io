Short Bio

I am a Visiting Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Dharwad. My research focuses on modeling and analyzing networked systems and social networks, with an emphasis on signed-network modeling, synthetic network generation, network reconstruction, and data-driven methods for understanding complex relational structures.

Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Researcher at KAIST, Daejeon, South Korea. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from IIT Roorkee, where my work addressed challenges in signed networks, from synthetic generation to predictive modeling.
