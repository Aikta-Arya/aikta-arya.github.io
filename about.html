About me

I am a computer scientist working at the intersection of network science and computational social systems. My research is centered on understanding, reconstructing, and generating realistic networks for analysis and downstream applications.

Research interests

Networked systems; social network models; signed networks; synthetic network generation; triangle counting; link prediction; and network modeling.

Academic background

Ph.D. in CSE from IIT Roorkee, following an M.Tech. in CSE from NIT Kurukshetra and a B.Tech. in CSE from Uttar Pradesh Technical University.
